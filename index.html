🎥 Record Video
1
Set your camera directly behind the ball, lined up with the uprights. Keep it as steady as possible.
2
Tap Record. Press START before the snap and STOP after the ball lands.
3
Review the clip — tap Use Video to proceed to analysis, or Retake to re-record.
💡 Tip: Film from 10–20 yards behind the ball at ground level for best accuracy.
⚙️ Kick Setup
1
Set Camera Depth — how far behind the ball your camera was placed (in yards).
2
Choose Football Type (NFL 11" / CFB 10.5" / Custom) — used to calibrate ball size in the frame.
3
Choose Upright Type (NFL 18.5ft / CFB 23.4ft / Custom) — sets the target window for Good From calculations.
4
Select Hash Position (Left / Middle / Right) — adjusts the lateral tolerance window for your kick location.
5
Toggle Uprights Visible if the goal posts appear in your video — this enables a camera angle correction step.
📐 Analyze Video — Step by Step
1
Scale Dots: Tap two points on the ground exactly 10 yards apart in the forward direction (toward the uprights) — e.g. two consecutive yard line markings near the ball. These calibrate real-world distance.
2
Ball Size: Pause on a still frame just before the kick. Tap the top then the bottom of the ball. The midpoint becomes the impact baseline.
3
Uprights (optional): If visible, scrub to the impact frame and tap the left then right upright post. This corrects for camera offset from center.
4
Half-Up: Scrub to roughly halfway through the ball's rise. Tap the ball.
5
Apex: Scrub to approximately the highest point of the ball's arc. Tap the ball.
6
Half-Down: Scrub to roughly halfway through the ball's descent. Tap the ball.
7
Landing: Scrub to the frame where the ball hits the ground (or nets). Tap the ball.
💡 Navigation: Use the zoom slider or pinch (mobile) to zoom in. To move around — tap & drag on mobile, click & drag on laptop. Use -1 Fr / +1 Fr to step frame by frame.
📊 Reading the Report
•
Kick Distance: Predicted total distance the ball traveled in yards.
•
Good From: The maximum distance this kick would be good from, given its drift and height profile.
•
Drift Profile: Whether the ball drifted Left, Right, or flew Straight through the uprights.
•
Drift at Landing: Exact lateral distance (in yards) from upright center at predicted landing point.
•
Hang Time: Total time the ball was in the air from impact to landing.
•
Peak Height: Maximum height the ball reached in feet.
•
Launch Angle: The angle of the ball's trajectory off the foot in degrees.
